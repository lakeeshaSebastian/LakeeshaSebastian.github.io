About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Mathematics & Statistics at Texas Tech University. My primary area of specialization is statistics, particularly reliability analysis and stress–strength reliability modeling.

My academic interests combine statistical research with undergraduate mathematics and statistics education. I have experience teaching a variety of undergraduate courses in mathematics and statistics and enjoy helping students develop conceptual understanding, mathematical reasoning, and confidence in quantitative subjects.

Research

Research Interests

  • Reliability Analysis
  • Stress–Strength Reliability
  • Statistical Inference
  • Bootstrap Methods
  • Simulation-Based Statistical Methods

Teaching

My teaching philosophy emphasizes student engagement, conceptual understanding, individual support, and appropriate use of technology to help students understand mathematical and statistical concepts.

At Texas Tech University, I have independently taught undergraduate mathematics and statistics courses in both face-to-face and online instructional settings.
